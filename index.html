Learning Through Reflection and Relationships

A journey of growth, learning, and meaningful connections.

Sometimes learning begins with answers, but other times it begins with listening. This portfolio is a reflection of my journey throughout this course, the ideas that have challenged me, and the experiences that continue shaping me as an educator and as a person.

As an immigrant educator from Colombia living in British Columbia, I have learned that teaching is much more than academic content. Teaching is also connection, empathy, respect, patience, and understanding the stories students bring with them into the classroom every day.

Through these modules, I hope to continue growing professionally while reflecting more deeply on how relationships, identity, and respectful learning environments can positively impact students and communities.
