Module 2 - Power of Indigenous Kinship

To build community and relationships in the classroom, it is vital to create an environment where students feel included, valued, and, most importantly, respected.

I have always believed that all human beings feel more comfortable in an inclusive environment when they know they belong. This is certainly true in the classroom, and when fostered from the earliest years of life, learning becomes more meaningful because it is based not only on academics but also on emotional development,

reinforcing the importance of interpersonal relationships.

I like to create spaces in the classroom where students feel heard, not only academically but also by sharing their interests and personal experiences,activities they have done, or who they spend time with outside of school. With this approach, I aim to promote empathy, teamwork, and valuing the voices of others. Small gestures like greeting students, asking how their day was, if they slept well, or even what they had for breakfast, make them feel at home, not just in a physical space where they attend classes to learn new things.

We live in a world where relationships aren't based solely on person-to-person interaction but also include community. How we relate to our environment and how we care for the resources nature provides generates a collective responsibility. I would like to continue incorporating these ideas, as well as creating collaborative

learning opportunities where students support each other instead of competing to see who is better. Likewise, I would like to further strengthen the practice of outdoor activities based on inquiry that foster stronger connections with nature. A couple of years ago, I had the good fortune to fill in for a teacher at a school called Environmental School. What a wonderful experience! I never imagined I would enjoy it so much. Seeing the students interact with nature and their understanding of the environment was incredibly enriching. I can honestly say that my life was transformed by that experience with that educational model.

These videos and readings also reminded me that, as educators, we play a fundamental role in modeling empathy, kindness, and healthy relationships. Strengthening the bonds with students and their families helps create a classroom community where everyone feels supported and motivated to grow together.
